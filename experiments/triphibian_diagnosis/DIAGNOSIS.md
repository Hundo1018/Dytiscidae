# Why no machine does the three-media mission, and a plan for at most three runs

2026-11-11, commit a7770c2. No search and no learner was run for this file.

Evidence: ten investigator reports in `reports/` (A1-A10), each claim there
with its `path:line`; raw-data tables regenerated by `scripts/a8_*.py`. The
predictions written before the evidence was read are in `PREREG.md` and are
compared in §3. E0, E0n and E0m (§5) are the new measurements, each with its
own frozen file under `existence/`. A fresh-context review
(`reports/R1_review.md`) checked fifteen claims of an earlier draft of §1-§3
(ten confirmed, three overstated, two wrong) and the plan; its corrections
are applied here and listed in §9. What the lead re-read at source is in
`NOTES.md`.

The user's constraint (2026-11-10): no new training until the analysis
supports a plan that reaches normal triphibian locomotion within three full
runs.

## 1 Verdict

The mission has read zero from arch34 to arch60 because six things held at
once, in every run. No run removed any of them, so the stored data cannot say
which would have been enough alone. E0 (§5) removed four of them together, by
hand, and one body then did every part of the mission under the repo's own
scores, with one qualification on land.

Model limits that bear on the plan (A3, A9): the buoyancy genes act in Tier-0
only (`fluid.py:1405` uses a fixed volume); rotors cannot reverse; no seed
plan carries a rotor; the rotor model is validated as a static thrust
coefficient in air only (rms 0.012 against the APC 10x4.7, camber fitted) and
not in water or at the surface, which is where E0 found the fragile manoeuvre.

How they connect. The parts of the mission the record shows being done on
command are few: water and land locomotion on nine arch51 elites each at the
recorded draw, air credit on 34 of 946 air-island elites, and two gliders'
`air_to_water`. Everything that needs leaving the ground or the water under
control is absent: no body the search holds can do it (2), nothing pays for
the steps toward it (3), and the mission that would reward the whole is
neither selected on nor measured, and at 8 s legs is in reach of a flying
machine only (1). Where a body can be steered, the policy has a few hundred
decisions on it and does not learn to read the command (4), and selection on
one draw does not keep what it finds (5).

What E0 adds, and what it does not. E0 removed four things together, by hand:
the body (a quad the genome can express, which no seed carries and Tier-0
rejects), the controller (a feedback cascade outside the action space), what
the controller reads, and the gate (a token fin). Under the unmodified scores
that machine flies, swims, makes all four crossings and both transitions of
the continuous mission from each start, and reads 0 on media and crossings
with its rotors stopped. This shows the set is jointly sufficient under a
hand law. It isolates none of the four, the search's own controller was not
run on that body, and its land result is a rotor-borne taxi, not weight on
the ground.

## 4 What the literature adds (reports A9, A10; each row was opened by the investigator, none re-opened by the reviewer)

- Every verified air+water+land robot is a 1.1-4.8 kg rotor platform with
  passive wheels or a rolling hull, near-neutral or negative buoyancy, and
  hand-designed mode logic over PID or MPC: TriphiBot 1.1 kg (arXiv
  2602.01385), the CUHK triphibian 4.76 kg (arXiv 2211.17022), Wukong-Omni
  4.4 kg (arXiv 2603.02602). None is learned. None walks: the land mode is
  wheels pushed by rotor thrust. None is shown crossing a shoreline on the
  ground. Take-off from floating needs air thrust-to-weight about 1.4-1.6.
- Nothing above about 0.3 kg flies and swims by flapping (FAAV, Science 2026,
  under 300 g, no turn, timed open loop); birds that use one wing in both
  media are flightless above about 1 kg (Elliott et al., PNAS 2013).
- Budgets on ONE fixed body: quadrotor from scratch about 3e5 steps
  (Eschmann et al., arXiv 2311.13081); flapping wing in MuJoCo with a
  stateless fluid model about 3e7 (Cai et al., arXiv 2411.15130); legged
  about 1.5e8 (Rudin et al., arXiv 2109.11978). Co-design gives each body
  5e5-5e6 steps (Evolution Gym, arXiv 2201.09863; DERL, arXiv 2102.02202).
  This project: 7e1-2e3 per body.
- One policy over many bodies works on fixed sets with body-aware networks
  and 1e6-1e8 steps per body (MetaMorph, arXiv 2203.11931; URMA, arXiv
  2409.06366). No report of one policy over a population that is still
  evolving.
- Chains of rare sub-goals: the evidence is for starting episodes near the
  goal from saved simulator states and moving the start back (Florensa et
  al., arXiv 1707.05300; Backplay, arXiv 1807.06919; DeepMimic's
  reference-state starts, arXiv 1804.02717; Go-Explore, arXiv 1901.10995).
- Under one evaluation per solution fewer than 20% of MAP-Elites elites sit
  in their true cell; the fix is re-evaluation (Extract-ME spends 25% of each
  batch, arXiv 2502.06585).

Where the plan departs from A10's own recommendation: A10 §4 spends run 1 on
per-skill RL experts (1e7-1e8 steps per skill, 1e8-1e9 for nine skills) and
records a distillation that lost over 40%. The plan below replaces the
experts with the hand law E0 already has, so run 1 is imitation plus
fine-tuning at 1e7 decisions, not exploration. That is cheaper and it caps
the student at the teacher (§6).

## 5 E0, E0n, E0m: the existence measurements

### E0 (`existence/PREREG.md` frozen first; `existence/RESULT.md`)

A quad-class body under one hand-written feedback law (the `fly.py` cascade,
a medium-aware rotor law, a depth target, a ground mode, a switch keyed to
the commanded medium), through the unmodified `run_segment`,
`run_transition` and `run_continuous`, seeds 0-7, beside the same body with
rotors stopped. No learning, no optimiser. 320 rollouts, 16.5 minutes, one
process, no `bad_qacc` in the hand arm. The lead re-ran seed 5 for both arms:
20 rollouts, 0 differences to the last digit.

Against its frozen text: five of seven predictions confirmed. Prediction 6
(nulls) is confirmed for media and crossings and refuted for the mission (the
score pays the still machine). Prediction 4's surface clause is refuted as a
general statement: no `bad_qacc` in the 320 final rows or the 64 entry-state
rows, but two tuning rollouts hit one and a third diverged, all on an exit
that was not level. Prediction 7 holds for the 24 s mission and fails for the
45-minute spec (0.87).

Limits of the design (review): the crossing and mission rows do not depend
on the seed (one entry state per kind), so they are n = 1; from eight entry
states B1 crosses 32 of 32 and B0 29 of 32. Seeds 0-3 were also the tuning
seeds. The only null was rotors stopped (E0n below adds three). Prediction 3
had no falsifier and prediction 7 was true by construction.

**"It can" stands for flight, swimming, the four crossings and both mission
transitions.** What E0 found on the way changes the plan:

1. **Tier-0 rejects a bare multirotor** (§1 row 6). No search could have held
   this body, seeded or not. B1 carries a token fin to pass.
2. **The search's aggregate misreads the working machine**: `mission_fraction`
   0.011-0.091 (0.21-0.55 without the leap-height factor). Nothing selects on
   it since arch51, and it would still be the largest value on record (the
   stored maximum is 0.0003).
3. **The mission score pays a machine doing nothing** 0.15-0.58, and credits a
   fall into the sea as a crossing. It cannot be the definition of done until
   it is gated.
4. **The land result is not land locomotion.** The land mode is a taxi 3 cm
   clear of the beach; dragging the feet at 94% thrust scores 0.39-0.87. No
   wheel is expressible (part hinges are range-limited,
   `core/mjcf.py:649-655`) and the body has no leg joints.
5. **The law reads what the policy cannot**: world position (so distance and
   bearing to the shore), heading, height over the ground, each rotor's
   wetness, the task phase and the clock, the model, the beach map
   (`RESULT.md` §5). It also keeps latches and timers (the staged exit, "sit
   until still, then taxi") that a memoryless policy has no place for.
6. **Leaving the water is the fragile manoeuvre**: it works staged and level.
7. Smaller: the quad's propellers are handed by side, so the mixer has rank 3
   and no yaw authority, and it flies and turns anyway; a heavier body swims
   better than a near-neutral one for a tilting-thrust machine;
   `run_transition` does not read `bad_qacc` on the single path.

Measured cost: 144 s of simulated time in 38 s of wall on one process (numpy
path, this body): 95 decisions per second, so 1e7 decisions is 29
process-hours.

### E0m: the same hand law on 32 one-step mutants of B1

One call each of the search's own operator (`core/genome.py:1234`, called as
at `evolution/loop.py:2128-2129` with the two-operator plan an early archive
draws, `evolution/curator.py:251-258`), `default_rng(20261111)`, the first 32
that changed the genome, none dropped. The **unchanged** hand law, 528
rollouts. The lead re-ran one mutant: 17 rows, 0 differences.

- **Tier-0: 31 of 32 pass.** The one rejection is the token fin losing its
  surface kind (`has_propulsor`).
- **Of the 29 passing children whose body changed, 15 complete the mission
  from all three starts (0.52; Wilson 95% 0.34-0.69)**, 12 make all four
  crossings (0.41), 9 do both. The frozen band is 0.3-0.7: "run 2 needs the
  law's model inputs in the policy and a per-child check before any
  fine-tune".
- Crossings lost, by kind: `water_to_land` 16, `water_to_air` 8,
  `air_to_water` 7, `land_to_air` 6. The prediction that `water_to_air` goes
  first is refuted, and so is "all four crossings on at least half". The
  prediction by density held: outside 1.02-1.10, 11 of 14 lose a crossing;
  inside, 6 of 15.
- All three children whose rotor count changed (to 3, 5 and 8) lost
  everything. Two more are outside the law (new joint motors; no rotor).
- Still arm: 0 of 124 crossings.

So run 2's premise holds for about half the children, under a controller

That is: the target is a flapping triphibian. One wing, designed directly and from references, has to row or fly the body through water, flap it through air, and carry it on land by supporting the body. Its shape, vibration, membrane or feathers, compliance and motion are free. Energy efficiency is not counted. Body generation and reinforcement learning are to be traded off, not assumed. This supersedes §7b's ordering (search as the goal) and §7a's rotor-borne anchor as the route; the instruments (crossing gates, `evaluate_mission`, `ground_support`) and the method stand.
