## Modelling decisions the spec did not fix
- Mesh: own structured meshes in numpy (not gmsh): S8R shell grids, chord line at 25% is a grid line. Axes: span x, chord y (LE at -c/2), camber up z. Arc: circular, chord 0.258 m, rise 5.8% of chord = 14.96 mm, R = 0.560 m. Constant chord, 0.70 m, root x=0 clamped.
- Linear `*STATIC`. Units SI.
- Plate (carbon sheet): S8R, `*ELASTIC, TYPE=ENGINEERING CONSTANTS` E1=E2=E3=60 GPa, nu=0.05 (all), G12=G13=G23=4.5 GPa (an isotropic card cannot give E 60 / G 4.5: nu would be 5.7). `*ORIENTATION` local 1 = span. nu=0.05 is my choice (spec silent).
- Foam: S8R shell 6 mm thick, isotropic E 15 MPa, G 6 MPa so nu = 0.25 (foam-as-shell: it is a thin sheet; plate theory, no through-thickness solid).
- Tube alone (cases T, tool check 1): B32R beam elements with `SECTION=PIPE` (ccx expands them to C3D20R solids internally; ro, wall as listed). An isotropic card cannot hold E 120 / G 4.5, so two legs per tube: torsion run with E=11.0 GPa, nu=0.2222 (G=4.5 GPa exactly), bending run with E=120 GPa, nu=0.3 (G does not matter for a slender beam, L/d = 80). Torque: moment on the tip node (DOF 4). Bending load: p * chord (6.66 N/m at 25.8 Pa) as consistent nodal forces.
- Tube in TP and F: **equivalent thin-wall S8R shell tube**, rm = sqrt((ro^2+ri^2)/2), wall = (ro^2-ri^2)/(2 rm); this has exactly the real tube's area, J and I. 12 elements round (24 for the halved mesh). Orthotropic E=120 GPa along and round (spec gives one E), G=4.5 GPa, nu 0.05. Reason: beam-to-shell rotation ties via `*EQUATION` on DOF 4-6 had no effect in ccx (the plate did not follow the tube: tip uz of the plate was exactly 0 while the tube twisted), so the joint is built from shared nodes instead.
- Joint: the tube sits on the plate surface at 25% chord (axis at the surface normal offset rm from the arc point). The plate's own node line at 25% chord is the tube's generator nearest the plate (same node ids, 6 DOF continuous, normals parallel there). The plate continues on both sides of the tube. It is a rigid line bond, not a glue layer. For F the line is on the foam mid-surface.
- Roots: P, TP, F: everything clamped (all 6 DOF, plate root edge and tube root ring). Tool check 2 and the "rootfree" rows: root edge u_y=u_z=0 (section cannot rotate or translate in-plane), u_x free except the two corner nodes (rigid-body), shell rotations free: the root can warp. "plateRootFree" TP row: only the tube ring is clamped, the plate root edge is free (except the shared line).
- Torque: P: pure couple of tangential nodal forces on the tip edge nodes, proportional to (tributary length x distance to the length-weighted centroid), net force zero; no tie, the tip can warp. T: moment on the tip beam node. TP, F, shell tube: the same kind of couple on the tube tip ring only (torque goes in through the spar; the plate takes its share by the bond). Torque 1.0 N m, linear (twist of the soft plates is far beyond any small angle; those numbers are linear-theory values).
- Twist = rotation of the tip section about the span axis: least-squares rotation of the in-plane displacement (u_y,u_z) of all .frd nodes at x=L (the expanded shell/beam nodes), after subtracting the mean translation and about the section centroid: theta = sum(y'(uz-mean) - z'(uy-mean)) / sum(y'^2+z'^2). For TP/F `twist_rad` uses the tube ring nodes only (0.5 rm < r < 1.5 rm); `twist_plate_tip_rad` is the plate's. GJ = T L / twist (spec definition); `GJ_local_x50_90` = T (0.4 L)/(twist(0.9L) - twist(0.5L)); `J_geom_m4` = GJ / G (per unit G, G=4.5 GPa; for F it is per G_carbon and tube-dominated).
- Pressure: spec load as written, 8.6 x 3 = 25.8 N/m^2, **and** the weight-consistent 0.60 kg x 9.81 / 0.34 m^2 x 3 = 51.9 N/m^2 (the spec's "8.6" is half of its own 17.3; both rows are in results.jsonl, linear so they scale 2.01x). Plate/foam: `*DLOAD P` on all shell elements (sign not controlled, magnitudes read). Tube alone: line load p x chord. "Tip deflection" = |mean u_z| over the tip section (`tip_defl_chordmean_m`); for tube-containing shells also the tube ring mean (`tip_defl_tube_axis_m`). T rows: the beam tip.
- Mass per wing: rho x mesh area x t (+ tube: rho pi (ro^2-ri^2) L). The wing here is a constant chord, 0.1806 m^2 projected (arc area 0.1822), so a pair is 0.361 m^2, not the spec's 0.34; `pair at 0.34 m^2` numbers below rescale the sheet only.
- Stress: nodal values from the .frd; `tau_max_mid_Pa` is half the largest principal difference over 0.15 L < x < 0.85 L (avoids the clamp and the load tip).

## Predictions (spec wording quoted, then the numbers)
**S1.** "The plate alone is far too soft in twist: 0.5 mm gives GJ about G c t^3/3 = 0.048 N m^2, a twentieth of what W7 asks, and 1 N m would twist it by more than a full turn in linear theory; the clamped root's warping restraint and the camber raise it by less than a factor 3. At 1.0 mm (0.39 N m^2 open-section) it is still under 1.17 and the pair of wings would weigh 0.53 kg."
Measured, clamped root (the spec's boundary): 0.3 mm GJ 0.266 (x25.5 the open-section value), 0.5 mm 0.497 (x10.3; twist 1.41 rad = 80.7 deg at 1 N m, not more than a turn), 1.0 mm 1.382 (x3.6; **above** 1.17). Root free to warp: 0.01051, 0.04858, 0.3879 (= G c t^3/3 to 0.6%). Local rate between 0.5 L and 0.9 L: 0.203, 0.379, 1.057. Mass per wing 0.0847, 0.1412, 0.2824 kg; pair 0.565 kg at 1.0 mm (0.527 at 0.34 m^2). **Fails** as worded (the factor-3, the full-turn and the "still under 1.17" parts); holds for the open-section values only with a free root. Cause (not in the spec): a cambered open section with a clamped root has a large warping constant; Vlasov fit to the 0.5 mm result gives lambda L about 0.6, i.e. the cantilever is warping-restraint dominated, not St Venant dominated, so the tip twist is not a torsion constant. The 25x at 0.3 mm shows the effect scales as t^-2.
**S2.** "The 8x6 tube alone gives 1.24 N m^2 (within 10%), at 25 g a side; 6x4 gives 0.46, 10x8 gives 2.6. Shear stress at 1 N m in the 8x6 tube is about 15 MPa."
GJ 1.2361 / 0.4592 / 2.6054; mass 24.6 / 17.6 / 31.7 g. Shear: closed form 14.5 MPa, shell tube 14.7 MPa; the beam model's nodal stress reads 20.6 MPa (28.9 vs 41.6 for 6x4: a constant 1.4x, the extrapolation of ccx's coarse pipe section; not trusted). **Holds** (GJ, mass; stress by closed form and shell model).
**S3.** "With the tube, the 0.3 mm plate adds under 15% to GJ; the pair of wings (two tubes, two plates) weighs 0.21 kg, a third of the machine."
TP (plate root clamped, the spec's case): GJ 1.7073 vs shell tube alone 1.2607: **+35.4%** (vs beam tube 1.2361: +38%); mesh-halved 1.7083. The plate lags the tube at the tip (plate tip twist 0.13 vs tube 0.41 rad at 1 N m: its warping is restrained). With the plate root free, GJ 1.2705: +0.8%. Mass per wing 0.1094 kg; pair 0.219 kg (0.207 with 0.34 m^2 sheet), i.e. 0.35-0.37 of 0.60 kg. **Fails** on the 15% as specified (clamped); holds if the plate's root is not clamped; mass holds (0.21 within 5%).
**S4.** "Depron on the tube adds under 5% to GJ and weighs 0.05 kg a side with the tube."
F: GJ 1.4018 vs shell tube alone 1.2607: **+11.2%** (vs beam: +13.4%). The foam alone (6 mm, G 6 MPa, clamped) is 0.1223 (open section G c t^3/3 = 0.1115): the prediction's mistake is that a 6 mm sheet has t^3 large enough to make 9% of the tube. Mass 0.0684 kg a side (foam 0.0437 + tube 0.0246), not 0.05. **Fails** both parts.
**S5.** "Under three times the weight's pressure the 8x6 tube alone (the plate carried by it, giving no bending stiffness of its own counted) deflects at the tip by 5-15% of the wing's length."
8x6 tube alone: 12.06 mm = **1.72% of L** at the spec's 25.8 N/m^2; 24.3 mm = **3.47% of L** at the weight-consistent 51.9 N/m^2 (closed form q L^4/8EI agrees to 0.4%; 6x4: 4.6% / 9.3%; 10x8: 0.8% / 1.6%). **Fails** for 8x6 under either load (5% needs about 75 N/m^2). Not part of S5 but counted in the model: the cambered 0.3 mm plate on the tube cuts the tube-axis tip deflection to 0.55 mm (1.11 mm at 51.9), because the cambered plate's own bending stiffness (E 60 GPa) is larger than the tube's; foam on the tube (F): 11.0 mm / 22.2 mm.

## Problems and corrections (all before any prediction comparison)
- B32 node order: ccx wants start, middle, end (first try had start, end, middle: nonpositive Jacobian).
- .frd reader first mistook element records for node records; fixed.
- `*EQUATION` ties of beam rotation (DOF 4) to shell nodes/rotations did nothing in ccx 2.23 (plate stayed at zero displacement); replaced by the shared-node shell tube (above). Before: beam tube GJ in the joined model 1.238 (plate not following); after: 1.7073.
- Beam expanded-section nodal stresses overshoot the closed form by 1.4x; shell tube matches it.
- ccx writes one stress value per expanded node; peak stresses for the soft plates at 1 N m are linear-theory numbers at 800 deg of twist and mean nothing.
- Not done (spec section 3 lists most): no buckling, no film tension, no joint to the shoulder; no solid (3-D) foam; the plate root clamp is the only plate boundary besides the free-root rows; no sensitivity of the bond width; the twist-axis position of the tube is fixed at its own geometric axis; no variation of the sheet's nu or of the tube's hoop modulus.
